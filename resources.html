Fabrication Best Practices

Plywood Storage & Handling Best Practices

Flat Horizontal Stacking

Always store plywood horizontally on flat, level wooden runners spaced no more than 60 cm apart. Never lean full sheets diagonally against walls for long periods, as uneven gravity causes curvature.

Job-Site Acclimatization

Allow newly delivered plywood panels to rest flat in the installation space for 48 hours before sawing and fabrication so the moisture content balances with ambient humidity.

Cut Edge Sealing

When cutting Marine or Film Faced plywood on site, always coat the freshly cut raw edges with waterproof paint or sealer to prevent capillary water intake into the inner plies.
