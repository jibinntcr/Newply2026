Our Heritage

Precision Engineering with Traditional Timber Expertise

Established in Perumbavoor, Kerala — widely recognized as South India's epicenter for timber processing and plywood manufacturing — Newtech Ply & Boards has built a solid reputation over two decades of dedicated production.

Our leadership and technical team combine more than fifty years of cumulative industry experience in veneer grading, adhesive chemical formulation, and high-pressure hot-press operation. We believe that true quality lies in consistency — ensuring that every sheet leaving our mill possesses uniform thickness, balanced moisture, and zero core delamination.

Operating Principles

The Pillars of Our Credibility

Uncompromised Bonding Quality

We never cut corners on adhesive resin quantities or press cycles. Our Marine grades undergo full 72-hour boiling water delamination tests in our in-house facility.

Prompt Supply & Order Fulfillment

Direct mill operation enables us to fulfill contractor truckloads, retail container lots, and customized thickness runs with dependable lead times.

Transparent Specifications

We provide clear, honest classifications: BWR is clearly distinguished from Marine BWP, and core compositions are transparently detailed for every grade.
