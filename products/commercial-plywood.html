Product Overview & Material Description

Newtech Commercial Plywood is an interior-grade board manufactured strictly in compliance with Indian Standard IS:303 (MR Grade). It is crafted from selected hardwood and plantation timber veneers bonded with synthetic amino resin adhesives under high temperature and hydraulic pressure.

The cross-laminated structure ensures balanced internal stress, preventing warping, cupping, and twisting common in solid lumber. Each sheet undergoes dual-head calibrated sanding to provide a clean, smooth, uniform face ready for laminates, wood veneers, paint, or polish.

Technical Specifications

Standard Specification IS:303 (Moisture Resistant - MR Grade)
Core Construction Selected Plantation Timber & Hardwood Core Veneers
Adhesive / Resin Fortified Urea Formaldehyde (Amino Resin)
Standard Thicknesses 4 mm, 6 mm, 9 mm, 12 mm, 16 mm, 18 mm, 25 mm (±0.5 mm tolerance)
Standard Dimensions 8 ft × 4 ft (2440 × 1220 mm), 7 ft × 4 ft (2140 × 1220 mm), 7 ft × 3 ft
Moisture Content 8% to 12% at dispatch
Face Finish Dual-head calibrated smooth sanded finish
Screw-Holding Strength High holding capacity on both surface and edges

Office Partitions

Commercial office dividers, cubicle work desks, and decorative wall lining.

Industrial Packaging

Protective cargo crates, packaging cases, and pallet structural reinforcement.

Key Handling & Storage Guidelines

  • Store sheets horizontally flat on level runners in a well-ventilated dry space.
  • Allow panels to acclimatize to local ambient humidity before fabrication.
  • Not recommended for direct, continuous outdoor exposure or prolonged standing water (use Newtech Marine IS:710 for wet zones).
