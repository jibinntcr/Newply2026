Product Overview & Water Resistance Engineering

Newtech Marine Plywood is formulated and manufactured strictly according to IS:710 specifications. It represents the highest echelon of water-resistant plywood manufacturing. Every core and face veneer is bonded using 100% unextended Phenol Formaldehyde (PF) resin, creating a thermoset cross-linked polymer lattice that is impervious to boiling water, steam, fungi, and bacterial breakdown.

Prior to assembly, core veneers undergo deep vacuum pressure impregnation with anti-termite and anti-borer preservative salts. When subjected to the official IS:710 laboratory test — continuous boiling at 100°C for 72 consecutive hours — Newtech Marine Plywood displays zero delamination, zero edge blister, and full retention of structural glue shear strength.
