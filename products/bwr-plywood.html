BWR Plywood (Boiling Water Resistant)

Manufactured with exterior phenolic adhesives to endure cyclic humidity, steam, and intermittent water contact without delamination.

Accurate Technical Classification

Newtech BWR Plywood is manufactured in strict accordance with IS:303 (BWR Grade) standards. Unlike interior MR plywood which is bonded with urea formaldehyde, BWR plywood is bonded using high-polymerization Phenol Formaldehyde (PF) synthetic resin.

Under laboratory testing, Newtech BWR Plywood survives 8 consecutive hours of immersion in boiling water without experiencing veneer separation, blister formation, or edge delamination. It is designed specifically for kitchen overhead units, dining furniture, internal door paneling, and semi-exposed architectural elements where moisture is present intermittently.

Specification Clarification: BWR grade is tested to withstand 8 hours of boiling water, providing robust protection for moisture-prone interior and semi-exterior spaces. For submerged, marine, or 72-hour continuous boiling proof needs (BWP), specify Newtech Marine Plywood (IS:710).
