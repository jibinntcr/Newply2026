Product Overview & Core Architecture

Newtech Alternate Plywood is manufactured through a specialized cross-lamination layup where alternating plies of dense hardwood veneers and selected plantation timber are bonded sequentially. This engineering balance reinforces overall panel tensile strength and impact durability while keeping the panel lighter and more compliant for woodworking machinery.

Carpenters and interior fabricators favor Alternate Plywood because it cuts with minimal blade chatter, takes screws cleanly without pre-drilling splits, and maintains calibrated flatness across large wardrobe doors and modular furniture panels.
