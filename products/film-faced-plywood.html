Formwork Performance Engineering

Newtech Film Faced Shuttering Plywood is a specialized structural panel manufactured specifically for concrete formwork applications. The core is constructed with selected hardwood and dense plantation veneers bonded with thermoset phenolic resin under intense hydraulic pressure.

Both outer surfaces are thermal-bonded with a 120 GSM phenolic resin impregnated film (dark brown mirror finish). All four perimeter edges are precision machine-sealed with water-resistant acrylic coating to prevent moisture absorption and edge swell during casting and wet curing. This results in ultra-smooth, fair-faced concrete surfaces, minimizing post-pour plastering requirements.

Technical Specifications

Surface Overlay Phenolic resin impregnated film (120 GSM, smooth gloss)
Edge Sealing Water-resistant acrylic emulsion seal on all four sides
Core Bonding 100% Phenol Formaldehyde (PF) synthetic resin
Chemical Resistance Highly resistant to concrete alkaline water and demoulding oils
Standard Thicknesses 12 mm (30 kg & 34 kg variants), 18 mm (custom heavy lots)
Standard Dimensions 8 ft × 4 ft (2440 × 1220 mm)
Repetition Cycles Designed for multiple repetitive concrete pours when properly maintained

Primary Construction Applications

Slabs & Decking

Cast-in-situ floor slabs and commercial podium decking requiring uniform finish.

Columns & Beams

Heavy civil column shuttering, sheer walls, and retaining wall forms.

Bridges & Flyovers

Infrastructure projects including bridge pier caps, box culverts, and parapets.

Precast Concrete Yards

Repetitive casting beds in precast concrete component manufacturing facilities.

Maintenance & Reusability Tips

  • Apply a quality demoulding oil or release agent before each concrete pour.
  • Always reseal newly cut edges with water-resistant acrylic paint to preserve edge integrity.
  • Clean concrete remnants promptly with water and a soft rubber scraper, avoiding wire brushes.
