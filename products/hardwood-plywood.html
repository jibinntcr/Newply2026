Product Overview & Mechanical Performance

Newtech Hardwood Plywood is constructed utilizing 100% selected dense tropical hardwood veneers throughout its cross-laminated core. The high inherent density of the species gives this panel outstanding modulus of elasticity (MOE), modulus of rupture (MOR), and resistance to physical deformation under heavy continuous loading.

Unlike softcore or lightweight panels, hardwood plywood offers unmatched screw-holding and nail-withdrawal resistance across both the face and core edges, making it the choice for demanding industrial and architectural requirements.

Technical Specifications

Core Composition 100% High-Density Tropical Hardwood Core Veneers
Adhesive / Resin Fortified High-Polymer Resin Adhesive
Density 700 – 850 kg/m³
Standard Thicknesses 9 mm, 12 mm, 16 mm, 18 mm, 25 mm (±0.5 mm)
Standard Dimensions 8 ft × 4 ft (2440 × 1220 mm), 7 ft × 4 ft
Bending Strength (MOR) High flexural modulus across both directions
Screw-Holding Strength > 250 kg on face, > 180 kg on edges
Moisture Content 8% to 12% stabilized

Primary Applications

Mezzanine Flooring

Heavy Door Carcasses

High-capacity export packaging designed to support heavy machinery without base deflection.
