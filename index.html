Material Anatomy

The Science of Cross-Lamination

Solid wood naturally expands and contracts across its grain with changes in humidity. Plywood neutralizes this movement by gluing consecutive veneer layers with their grain orientation rotated at 90 degrees.

Two-Way Tensile Distribution

Forces applied anywhere on the sheet are shared along both axes, multiplying impact resistance.

Zero Longitudinal Splitting

Fasteners, screws, and nails can be driven close to board edges without the splitting common in solid timber.

Hydraulic Hot Press Bonding

Phenolic resins polymerize under extreme thermal pressure to form permanent, waterproof molecular bonds.

Production Excellence

6-Stage Calibrated Manufacturing Process

From sustainable plantation log peeling to 72-hour boiling water testing, our workflow enforces rigorous quality checks at every station.

01

Log Grading & Rotary Peeling

Selected round logs are debarked, steam-conditioned, and precision-peeled into continuous ribbon veneers with uniform calibrated thickness.

02

Controlled Veneer Core Drying

Green veneers pass through automated jet dryers to stabilize moisture content between 6% and 8%, preventing internal warp and blister defects.

03

Resin Spreading & Cross-Plying

Controlled glue spreaders coat core veneers with formulated Phenol / Urea adhesives. Plies are manually assembled in alternating 90° grain orientation.

04

High-Pressure Hydraulic Hot Pressing

Pre-pressed assemblies enter multi-daylight hydraulic presses operating under controlled temperatures (up to 150°C) and pressure (12–15 kg/cm²).

05

Trimming & Dual-Head Sanding

Cured panels are square-trimmed with diamond-tipped saws and calibrated through high-precision wide-belt sanders for a smooth, even surface.

06

Rigorous In-House Quality Testing

Every batch is tested in our laboratory for glue shear strength, moisture content, retention, and 72-hour boiling water delamination tests.

Environmental Stewardship

Responsible Manufacturing Practices

We prioritize plantation timber sourcing and comprehensive wood-waste recycling throughout our Perumbavoor facility.

Plantation Timber Sourcing

We utilize sustainably harvested plantation timbers such as rubberwood and agro-forestry species, minimizing pressure on natural primary forest habitats.

100% Wood Residue Biomass Utilization

All wood trimmings, peeling cores, and sawdust are directed into our closed-loop thermal boiler to generate the steam energy required for hot-pressing.

Low-Emission Bonding Formulation

Our adhesive blending systems strictly adhere to resin polymerization protocols to minimize free formaldehyde emissions for safer indoor air quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

BWR (Boiling Water Resistant) plywood complies with IS:303 and is bonded with exterior synthetic resin capable of withstanding up to 8 hours in boiling water. Marine plywood complies with the stringent IS:710 standard, uses 100% unextended phenol formaldehyde resin, and must survive 72 hours of continuous boiling water testing without delaminating. Marine grade is essential for wet areas such as bathrooms and under-sink kitchen cabinets.
