Architectural Interiors

Residential Furniture, Wardrobes & Paneling

For living rooms, master bedrooms, and office suites, dimensional stability and surface smoothness determine the finish quality of premium laminates, veneers, and PU coatings. Newtech Commercial and Alternate grades provide flat, calibrated surfaces free from core undulations.

  • Wardrobes & Storage: Calibrated 18 mm and 12 mm panels resist warping under door weight.
  • Bed Frames & Living Units: High flexural stiffness preventing sag over wide spans.
  • Wall Paneling & Ceilings: 6 mm and 9 mm lightweight sheets for acoustic and decorative lining.

High-Moisture Environments

Modular Kitchens, Bathrooms & Coastal Spaces

Kitchens and washrooms expose wood panels to cyclic humidity, steam, water splashes, and plumbing condensation. Standard interior boards rapidly absorb moisture, leading to delamination and fungal rot. Newtech Marine (IS:710) and BWR grades provide guaranteed protection.

  • Under-Sink Cabinets: Marine IS:710 with 72-hr boiling water resistance survives plumbing leaks.
  • Upper Kitchen Cabinets: BWR grade protects against stove heat and steam condensation.
  • Vanities & Bathrooms: Preservative-treated core veneers repel mold and moisture.

Structural Heavy Loading

Transport Flooring, Container Decks & Industrial Mezzanines

Freight transport, bus passenger floors, and industrial mezzanine walkways subject wood panels to severe point loads, forklift movement, and continuous vibration. Newtech 100% Hardwood Plywood provides high modulus of rupture (MOR) to safely distribute concentrated axle and foot traffic.

  • Container Flooring: High shear strength resisting pallet movement and shifting cargo.
  • Bus Body Floors: Vibration-damped hardwood core resisting flexural fatigue.
  • Mezzanine Walkways: High point-load tolerance over structural steel joists.

Civil Construction Formwork

Concrete Shuttering, Slabs & Heavy Packing

Civil infrastructure and multi-story construction demand shuttering panels that withstand wet concrete hydrostatic pressure, vibrator consolidation, and alkaline concrete attack without face pitting. Newtech Film Faced Shuttering Plywood delivers smooth architectural finishes across repeated casting cycles.

  • Fair-Faced Concrete Slabs: 120 GSM phenolic film leaves mirror-smooth ceiling surfaces.
  • Beams & Retaining Walls: Sealed edges eliminate water absorption from wet concrete.
  • Machinery Crating: Heavy-duty timber cases protecting precision export machinery.
