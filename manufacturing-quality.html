Quality Philosophy

Engineered for Structural Integrity

The strength of a plywood panel is determined before it ever reaches the hot press — in the precise drying of green veneers, the balanced selection of hardwood core plies, and the chemical formulation of its resin bond.

At our Perumbavoor manufacturing mill in Kerala, every process is calibrated to eliminate internal core gaps, thickness variations, and uneven moisture gradients. Our panels conform strictly to Bureau of Indian Standards specifications including IS:303 and IS:710.

Step-by-Step Workflow

The 6-Stage Manufacturing Workflow

01

Log Conditioning & Rotary Peeling

Selected round timber logs are cross-cut, debarked, and conditioned with steam. High-speed precision rotary lathes peel the timber into continuous ribbons of veneer with uniform calibrated thickness.

02

Controlled Jet Drying

Freshly peeled green veneers contain 50% to 70% water. Continuous-feed mechanical jet dryers reduce and equalize the moisture content to a stabilized 6% – 8% to ensure flawless adhesive penetration.

03

Resin Spreading & Cross-Plying

Core veneers pass through mechanized four-roll glue spreaders applying calculated resin spreads. Skilled assembly teams lay the veneers with alternating 90° grain orientation around a solid center core.

04

Hydraulic Pre-Press & Hot Pressing

Assembled sheets first undergo cold pre-pressing to consolidate the glue line, then enter heavy multi-daylight hydraulic hot presses operating at temperatures up to 150°C and pressures of 12–15 kg/cm².

05

Precision Sizing & Dual Sanding

Hot-pressed boards cool to relieve internal curing stress. Automatic double-dimension saws square-trim all four edges. Panels then feed through dual-head wide-belt calibration sanders for smooth faces.

06

Quality Assurance & Lab Testing

Sample specimens from every production batch are tested in our in-house lab for glue shear strength, 72-hour boiling water delamination (for Marine grade), moisture content, and surface tolerance.

Rigorous Testing

In-House Laboratory Test Protocols

We believe that documented quality is the foundation of client confidence. Our test facility carries out standard mechanical and environmental stress tests:

  • 72-Hour Boiling Water Delamination Test (IS:710) Test specimens are submerged in boiling water at 100°C for 72 continuous hours, followed by rapid water quench, to confirm zero veneer separation.
  • Knife Shear & Glue Shear Test Evaluates the mechanical bond strength between adjacent plies. Shear failure must occur within the wood fibers rather than across the glue-line.
  • Moisture & Density Verification Electric moisture meters and digital thickness gauges ensure dispatch sheets remain within the specified 8% – 12% moisture range and ±0.5 mm thickness tolerances.
